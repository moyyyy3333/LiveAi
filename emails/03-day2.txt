Apply for the EIN on IRS.gov.

Not on a site that googles well for "get an EIN today."
Not through a dashboard that charges $79 to type the same form.
Not through anyone who needs your Social Security number in a chat box to "expedite."

The IRS issues EINs to new LLCs at no charge. If a page is selling you the number, it is selling you a printout of a free page.

When you file:

- Use the exact legal name from the state.
- The responsible party is a real principal. That is an IRS requirement. It is not Ignition collecting a Social Security number. We do not want it and we do not store it.

After the notice lands, put it in the export folder next to the formation document. You will need both for the bank.
