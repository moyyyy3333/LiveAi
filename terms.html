3. The 30-Day Trade-Credit Guarantee (Sprint Pass only)

This section applies only to customers who purchased the Sprint Pass and have not received a prior refund on the same LLC.

3.1 What an "open credit line" means here

An open credit line means written vendor net-30 (or other invoice) terms in the legal name of the LLC, from one of the three Sprint vendors named in the product (or a substitute vendor we assign in writing if a named vendor stops granting new-entity terms). A customer login, a prepaid order, a "pay this invoice now and reapply later," a personal-guarantee card, a consumer credit card, or a line that appears only on a credit bureau later do not count. Trade terms from the vendor count even if Dun & Bradstreet has not yet posted them.

3.2 When the clock starts

The 30-day clock starts on the calendar date the LLC's business checking account is opened, provided pre-flight is complete that same day or earlier: (a) EIN issued in the LLC's exact legal name, and (b) the checking account is in that same legal name. If pre-flight is finished after the account exists, the clock starts the day pre-flight is marked complete in the desk.

3.3 What you must complete on schedule

To keep the guarantee, you must, by the desk deadlines (48-hour grace):

  1. Keep one exact legal name, one address, and one business phone on every filing and application (the NAP lock).
  2. Request a free D-U-N-S Number at dnb.com by day 4. Paid "faster DUNS" resellers void the guarantee.
  3. Submit the three Sprint vendor applications, unaltered in legal name / address / phone, on the days they are assigned.
  4. Place and pay any first orders the checklist requires from the LLC checking account, same day as the invoice.
  5. Send us the Proof Pack in Section 3.5 if you request a day-20 review or a refund.

Missing a deadline by more than 48 hours, changing the NAP mid-sprint, or submitting a personal Social Security number on a vendor form after the desk instructed you not to, voids the guarantee. The rest of the software still works. The refund does not.

3.4 What we do if nothing is approved by day 20

Email help@ignitiondesk.biz with the Proof Pack. Within two business days we return (a) a written note on the file and (b) one revised application packet. That is the entire day-20 intervention. We do not call vendors as you, we do not log into your accounts, and we do not file with the IRS, a bank, or D&B as you.

3.5 Proof Pack (required for review or refund)

Attach, redacting account numbers:

  • EIN issuance notice
  • First page of the business checking statement or opening letter, showing legal name and open date
  • DUNS request confirmation
  • Submitted copies or screenshots of the three vendor applications
  • Vendor replies, including denials and "pay now, apply later"
  • Proof of any required order and payment from the LLC account
  • The desk checklist with dates

No Proof Pack, no refund. Fabricated completions are a breach of acceptable use.

3.6 The remedy

If Sections 3.2–3.5 are satisfied and day 30 passes with no open credit line as defined in Section 3.1, email help@ignitiondesk.biz within 14 days after day 30. We refund the Sprint Pass fee in full within 10 business days to the original payment method. Pre-flight Kit fees are not covered by this guarantee.

3.7 What this guarantee is not

It is not a promise by any vendor, bank, or bureau. It is not a guarantee of a loan, a credit score, a Paydex, a funding amount, a D-U-N-S issuance date, or the date a bureau posts a tradeline. Anyone who guarantees a bank approval is selling something we refuse to sell.

3.8 Exclusions

The guarantee does not apply if the LLC is not a U.S. entity; if the address on the applications is a location the vendor rejects as non-deliverable and you declined a deliverable alternative; if you applied for consumer credit in a principal's name instead of trade terms in the LLC name; or if a named Sprint vendor has publicly stopped new-entity net-30 accounts and we assigned a substitute in writing which you did not submit.

Marketing summary (matches Section 3 exactly): If you complete the dated checklist on time — including the free DUNS request by day 4 and the three vendor applications — and the LLC has no written vendor net-30 terms in its own name 30 days after the business checking account opens, we refund $149. Prepaid orders and bureau posting dates do not count as the line. Day-20 help is a written file review, not a phone call to the vendor.

4. Refunds outside the guarantee

Preview is free; there is nothing to refund. Pre-flight Kit is refundable within 7 days if the full operating agreement has not been downloaded. Sprint Pass refunds outside the 30-Day Trade-Credit Guarantee are handled case by case and are not guaranteed.

Do not misrepresent completed steps to obtain a refund or a day-20 review. Do not use paid "credit privacy number" (CPN) products, seasoned-tradeline services, or paid DUNS-acceleration services in connection with a guarantee claim — these void the guarantee under Section 3.3.
