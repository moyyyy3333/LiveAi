The guarantee is a refund on the Sprint Pass. It is not a promise from any vendor, a bank, or D&B.

Scam warnings

What we refuse to sell you

Paid EIN "filing"

The IRS issues EINs to new LLCs at no charge on IRS.gov. If a site is charging to type your legal name into that form, you're paying for a printout of a free page.

"Faster DUNS" resellers

The D-U-N-S request on dnb.com is free. Paid resellers sell speed and formatting — and a second, messier identity next to the one you're trying to keep consistent.

Seasoned tradelines & CPNs

We don't sell seasoned lines, CPNs, or guaranteed bank approvals. Anyone who promises those is selling the exact scam this desk exists to keep you out of.
